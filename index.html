Questions

The things everyone asks.

Is this AI?

Yes. Every image and every clip we deliver is produced with AI tools, art directed by us and checked before it goes out. We are not going to pretend otherwise. It is the reason you get a shoot's worth of visuals in days, with no studio hire, no crew and no location fee.

Will it look fake?

That is the whole job. Your label has to be readable and correct. Light has to fall the way light actually falls. Glass, liquid, fabric and metal have to behave. We check every file at full size for the things that give bad AI images away, and if a shot does not hold up, we do not send it.

You also see the direction before production starts, and you get two rounds of revisions after delivery. Nothing goes live that you have not approved.

Do I need to ship you my product?

No. Send the photos you already have, or just the URL of your product page. The more angles and detail shots you can give us, the more accurate the result, but a product page is enough to start.

How many revisions do I get?

Two rounds, included on every piece. Revisions cover styling, lighting, background, framing and crop. Changing to a completely different product is a new piece.

Who owns the images?

You do. You get full commercial rights to everything we deliver, on final payment. Use them on your site, in your ads, on social and in print. We do not license them to anyone else.

How fast is it?

First delivery lands [X] days after you approve the direction, and revisions come back within [X] days. A rush is possible on most projects. Ask when you send your product.

What files do I get?

Full-resolution stills plus web-sized versions, in the crops you need for your product page, your ads and social. Video is delivered in the aspect ratios you ask for.

What if my product is difficult?

Some products are harder than others. Fine print, complex transparent packaging and intricate texture all take more work, and a few things are not worth doing this way at all. Send us the product page and we will tell you honestly before you pay anything.
